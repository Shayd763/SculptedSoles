8:15am Before the kettle’s boiled

Three steps, all from home.

No appointments and nothing to download. Here’s what happens.

Your footprint

  1. Step 1

    Take three photos

    Pop a sheet of A4 paper on the floor, stand on it and take three photos with your phone. We’ll guide you through each one. It all happens in your web browser, so there’s nothing to download. If bending down is tricky, someone else can take them for you.

  2. Step 2

    We map your feet

    The sheet of A4 gives us a known size to measure against. We use your photos to make a contour map of each foot, then design your insoles around the shape we find.

  3. Step 3

    Printed in the UK, posted to you

    Your insoles are 3D printed here in the UK and posted to your door. They slip into the shoes you already wear and love.
